meh-publisher

Private upload tool for the Professor Meh channels

meh-publisher is a small command-line tool used by the owner of the Professor Meh YouTube and Instagram channels. It uploads our own original animated videos to our own channels and schedules them for publication.

It runs on a single computer operated by the channel owner. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and it does not access any other person's account or data.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to our own YouTube channels and sets the scheduled publish time.
  • Attaches the subtitle files we produce ourselves.
  • Reads basic performance statistics of our own channels for a weekly review.

YouTube API Services

meh-publisher uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) to upload and schedule videos on our own YouTube channels, @mehprofessor and @profesormeh. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy.
