Privacy Policy

meh-publisher · Effective 10 November 2026

meh-publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Professor Meh channels to upload and schedule our own videos on our own accounts. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with that access.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only for the channel owner's own channels, meh-publisher:

  • uploads our videos and sets their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time;
  • uploads our own subtitle files;
  • reads the channel's name to confirm that the right channel is connected;
  • reads our own channels' performance reports (views, watch time and similar statistics) through the YouTube Analytics API.

Through the Instagram Graph API it publishes our videos to our own Instagram accounts and reads their statistics.

It does not access, collect or store any data about viewers or any other users.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Statistics are kept in local files on that computer for our own review. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and Meta's own APIs.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Local files are kept only as long as they are useful for running the channels and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page or in Meta's business settings.

YouTube API Services

meh-publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube and Google process data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Authorized YouTube data (the channel name and our own channels' analytics) is used only for the purposes above. Any analytics data stored locally is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, and all locally stored YouTube API data and tokens are deleted when access is revoked.

You can revoke meh-publisher's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. To ask for deletion of any stored data, email mehprofessor@gmail.com; we delete it within 7 days.
